Privacy policy

VantriqAI app, customer portal (portal.vantriqai.com) and survey pages · Last updated 27 September 2026

Vantriq AI ("VantriqAI", "we") is an AI-automation company in Islamabad, Pakistan. This policy explains what the VantriqAI app, the customer portal and the customer-satisfaction survey pages collect, why, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Businesses using the app or portal

  • Account details: company and contact name, email, phone, and the billing details you give us (such as NTN and address) — to run your account and issue invoices.
  • Sign-in: your username and password (stored only as a one-way hash) and a session token on your device.
  • Service records: usage of your AI agents, invoices, payments and contracts — to bill you and show you your account.

People answering a survey

  • Your answers to the survey, and any contact details you choose to type in (they are always optional).
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly to stop abuse (limiting how many answers one device can send) and to count page views; it is not stored with your answers.
  • If a survey link was sent to you after a WhatsApp conversation, your answer is linked to that conversation for the business that sent it.
  • Your answers go to the business that ran the survey; VantriqAI processes them on its behalf. For questions about a survey, contact that business, or us.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, and we do not sell personal data.
  • Your device keeps only what the app needs (your session, your language, an unfinished survey) in its own storage.

Who else handles data

  • Hosting on our servers with Hostinger; email notifications through Hostinger's mail service.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

Account and billing records for as long as your account is active and as tax law requires. Survey answers until the business deletes the survey or asks us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data by emailing support@vantriqai.com. The app is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.

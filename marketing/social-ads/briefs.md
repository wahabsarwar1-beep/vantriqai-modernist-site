# Post briefs

Drop ideas here for upcoming VantriqAI posts. Each week the content routine
reads this file, turns the briefs into finished cards and captions, schedules
them with a **Post on:** date in `captions.md`, and moves used briefs to *Done*.

Anything works: a topic, an offer, a client win you're allowed to share, an
event, a photo you want used (add it to this folder and name it here). If
nothing is listed, the routine picks topics from the website.

Format (one per line, date optional):

- 2026-10-10 · Carousel · "How a WhatsApp agent handles a sale from first message to payment"
- Single · Our new Voice Agent answers the business phone line
